## 🏗️ Featured Case Study

**Salesforce build for a property housing services provider.** 1,400+ properties, 25+ local authorities.

| 3 → 1 | 1,400+ | 25+ | 100+ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| systems consolidated | properties managed | councils served | daily users |

- 🔀 **Three systems into one:** migrated two legacy housing systems plus spreadsheets into a single org with a clean council account hierarchy
- 📋 **End-to-end booking lifecycle:** staged pipeline from available void to sign-out
- 🛡️ **Safeguarding flags:** dated duty-of-care banners on booking and property pages
- 🤖 **AI-assisted repairs triage:** public web-to-case flow with AI diagnostics before a visit is booked
- ✅ **Compliance automation:** scheduled flows that raise the next gas safety and other certificates
- 🌐 **Council self-service portal:** Experience Cloud visibility into bookings, certificates and standards
- 📊 **Void board dashboard:** live vacancy and revenue-impact view, on screen in the office every day
